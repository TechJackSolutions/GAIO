Enforcement Mode

This determines whether the AI treats all rules as mandatory or gives you flexibility on operational rules while keeping integrity rules locked. Mode is enforcement posture. It is independent of the configuration weight (token size) below.

Confirm Integrity Lock. Here is exactly what it removes.

Under Integrity Lock, these operational rules become advisory instead of enforced:

  • Scope boundaries: the AI may engage outside your configured focus (with a note) instead of hard-redirecting.
  • Escalation flags: "consult a professional" becomes an informational note instead of a mandatory flag.

What stays fully enforced: all anti-fabrication and accuracy rules, and omission integrity (the AI still may not answer a different question than asked without disclosure, or omit meaning-changing context). By confirming, you take on those operational decisions personally.

Configuration Weight

Token size of the generated configuration. Weight is packaging, not posture. Every weight retains all rule classes; smaller weights compress the language. Any rule class omitted for budget is disclosed in the generated header.
